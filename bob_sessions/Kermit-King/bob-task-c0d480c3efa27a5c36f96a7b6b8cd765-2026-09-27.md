## Why two URLs?

| Variable | Port | Used by | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DATABASE_URL` | 6543 | Prisma Client (runtime) | PgBouncer pooling — handles many concurrent connections efficiently |
| `DIRECT_URL` | 5432 | `prisma migrate` / `db push` | Migrations need a direct connection, not a pooler |

That's the full change — just the schema provider swap + two env vars. No code changes needed anywhere else.
